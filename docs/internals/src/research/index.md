# Research Results and Artifacts

Research artifacts preserve investigations, experiments, benchmarks, and
technical comparisons that remain useful independently of whether they led to
an adopted implementation.

These documents are historical snapshots rather than maintained descriptions
of the current code. Where possible, they identify the question investigated,
the relevant revision or environment, the approach taken, the findings, and
important limitations. An ADR may summarize and cite research that materially
informed a later decision.
